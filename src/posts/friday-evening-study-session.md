Friday evening, and I am deep into my study session. However, I have noticed that I am no longer able to learn anything new today. My brain feels overloaded. Instead of forcing myself to remember more, I decided to use this time to write a short post for my blog.

Today, I finished the module about AWS Aurora, RDS, and caching.

Before starting my sprint, I spent some time thinking about how I should manage my notes and how I could remember what I learned. Taking notes is one part of the process. Remembering them is another.

The setup that works for me is using Obsidian with the Zettelkasten method to gather my notes. Then, I open Excalidraw inside Obsidian and put the notes there. This allows me to see how the notes I created are connected and attach some images to them.

I am also taking advantage of the image creation tool in ChatGPT. I can describe the concept I am trying to understand and ask ChatGPT to create a visual explanation for me. I also guide it by explaining what I want to see in the image. Sometimes, I make a sketch myself and ask ChatGPT to polish it. This seems to be working pretty well.

![My visual notes about AWS Aurora, RDS, and caching](/images/aws-aurora-rds-study-notes-2026-11-20.png)

At the end of the day, I can look at all my notes. There are plenty of details, and I forget many of them almost immediately after studying. But that is okay for now.

The next step will be to transfer some of these notes into Anki cards. I will not transfer everything, only the concepts that might appear on the exam or that I consider important. This should help me retain the knowledge.
