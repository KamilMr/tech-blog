I also studied “Scope of Architecture Characteristics” from the book [*Fundamentals of Software Architecture*](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/fundamentals-of-software/9781492043447/ch07.html). I learned about architecture quanta. A quantum is an independently deployable part of a system, with its parts working toward one purpose. It includes the dependencies it needs to work, such as a database. In the example below, the service waits for the database, so a slow database also means a slow response from the service.
